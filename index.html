About

A young person once asked:

“Hey, 8x and 9x guys. I'm a Gen Z, but I'm really curious about what you went through when you saw the integration of the country. What was the atmosphere like back then? How did you feel when you first came into contact with the internet? Was it like how we are with AI now?”

And I answered:

“No. With integration, there was excitement for new things, and it came slowly—year by year. With AI, there’s confusion; this wave is coming too fast—month by month.”

I’ve been in the IT industry since 2015. I had the opportunity to try my hand as a leader, managing a test team that at one point grew to 15 people. It was an interesting but also stressful role, and it reaffirmed that I’m more suited to technology-focused work. In 2020, I fully transitioned into automation testing.

I worked in the IT industry during its peak—when it was one of the hottest fields. By 2022, I started thinking about the next major technological direction as software development was beginning to feel increasingly saturated.

In 2024, I began exploring that direction at EPITA. I remain interested in understanding how AI, machine learning and deep learning differ, how these technologies work, and how machines are trained to understand the world around us.

I am currently looking for opportunities where I can continue learning while contributing my experience in software quality, automation and data-driven technologies.
