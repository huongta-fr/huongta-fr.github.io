Generalizable Instance Segmentation under Visual Style and Domain Shifts

Research Internship | LIFO - Université d'Orléans | 2026
Duration: 6 months

In manga/comic storytelling, instance segmentation is one of the main factors for getting full information from the story and rebuilding it. However, a pretrained model on natural images does not give high-quality results on manga images because of domain/style shift. This internship investigates the current limitations of instance segmentation under visual style and domain shifts and establishes an experimental and data-processing foundation for future research on domain-robust segmentation.

This stage provides a reproducible benchmark for recent instance segmentation models, standardized data resources, and a reusable synthetic-data generation pipeline. This basic step can support future experiments to study segmentation robustness and generalization across unseen comic styles.

Key responsibilities & achievements

  • Dataset Preparation & Standardization: Collected, processed, and standardized manga/comic datasets, converting annotations into COCO format to support consistent training and evaluation.
  • Segmentation Model Benchmarking: Evaluated existing instance segmentation approaches, including Mask2Former and SAM, to assess their performance on manga and comic imagery.
  • Failure Analysis & Reproducibility: Investigated segmentation failure cases, domain-shift challenges, and reproducibility issues in model training and evaluation.
  • Generative AI Evaluation: Explored and evaluated generative AI techniques, including diffusion-based models, for synthetic image generation and instance mask creation.
  • Synthetic Data Generation Pipeline: Designed and implemented an automated image-mask generation pipeline leveraging Manga109 as the source dataset, Danbooru for diverse character assets, and FLUX-based pose generation to create new character variations. Integrated mask-aware character replacement while preserving manga layouts, producing 1,000 synthetic manga pages with COCO-compatible annotations.
